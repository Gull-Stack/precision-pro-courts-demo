Our Story

Built on Passion, Driven by Precision

Precision Pro Courts was founded with a simple mission: to build beautiful, high-performance sport courts that Utah families can enjoy for decades. What started as a passion for sports and craftsmanship has grown into Utah's most trusted court construction company.

We believe every family deserves a world-class court — and that it shouldn't cost a fortune. That's why we combine premium materials, expert installation, and transparent pricing to deliver outstanding value on every project.
