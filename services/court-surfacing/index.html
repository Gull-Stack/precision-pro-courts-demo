Installation Process

Professional Surface Installation

Our proven process ensures your court surface meets the highest standards for performance and longevity.

1

Surface Assessment

Thorough evaluation of existing surface condition, drainage, and preparation requirements.

2

Preparation & Repair

Surface cleaning, crack repair, and application of primer systems for optimal adhesion.

3

Base Coat Application

Professional spray application of base coats with proper thickness and uniform coverage.

4

Color Coat System

Multiple color coats applied with texture additives for consistent color and traction.

5

Line Marking

Precision line marking for all sports with durable line paint and protective top coating.

6

Final Inspection

Complete quality inspection and court readiness evaluation before project completion.

Long-Term Care

Surface Maintenance & Care
