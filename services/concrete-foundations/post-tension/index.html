Post-Tension Concrete Systems

Post-tension concrete represents the pinnacle of foundation technology for sport courts. This advanced system uses high-strength steel cables under tension to compress the concrete, creating a foundation with superior crack resistance, load distribution, and longevity — even in challenging soil conditions.

Superior Benefits:

  • Thicker slabs engineered for large court surfaces
  • Maximum crack control, even in shifting soil conditions
  • Excellent performance in expansive soils and freeze-thaw climates
  • No control joints needed — long, continuous spans
  • Superior durability and extended court lifespan
  • Reduced maintenance and fewer repairs over the years
  • Much flatter courts over time as the slab resists movement
  • Precision installation by specialists in post-tension systems
  • Industry-leading warranties for peace of mind

Technology Explained

How Post-Tension Works

Understanding the engineering behind this advanced foundation system.

🔧

Cable Installation

High-strength steel cables are laid in precise patterns before concrete pour, encased in protective sheathing.

🏗️

Concrete Pour

Concrete is poured around the unstressed cables, creating a monolithic slab with embedded tensioning system.

⚡

Tensioning Process

After concrete cures, hydraulic jacks stretch the cables to precise tension, compressing the concrete from within.

🛡️

Permanent Compression

The tensioned cables maintain continuous compression, preventing cracks and providing superior structural integrity.

Installation Process

Professional Post-Tension Installation

Our certified technicians follow strict protocols for post-tension system installation.

1

Engineering & Design

Structural engineering analysis and cable layout design for your specific site conditions.

2

Site Preparation

Precision excavation, base preparation, and installation of cable anchors and dead-ends.

3

Cable Installation

Careful placement of post-tension cables in protective sheaths according to engineering plans.

4

Concrete Pour

High-quality concrete placement with special attention to cable protection and surface finishing.

5

Tensioning & Grouting

Hydraulic tensioning to design loads, followed by protective grouting of cable ducts.

6

Quality Assurance

Complete testing and documentation to ensure system meets all engineering specifications.

Premium Choice

When to Choose Post-Tension

Post-tension concrete is the premium choice for courts that demand the highest performance and longevity. While it represents a larger initial investment, it often pays for itself through reduced maintenance and superior performance.

Recommended For:

  • Expansive clay or unstable soil conditions
  • Tennis courts and larger court areas
  • Commercial and high-use installations
  • Premium residential projects
  • Areas with extreme weather conditions
  • Projects requiring minimal joints
  • Long-term investment properties
  • When maximum crack resistance is critical

Investment Protection

While post-tension systems cost more upfront, they typically require less maintenance and provide longer service life, often resulting in lower total cost of ownership.

Technical Specifications

Post-Tension System Details

We use only the highest quality materials and follow strict engineering standards.

Cable Specifications

270 ksi seven-wire strands with protective coating, sized according to structural engineering requirements.

Concrete Strength

Minimum 4,000 PSI compressive strength with low shrinkage admixtures for optimal performance.

Slab Thickness

Typically 4-5" thickness due to superior strength characteristics of post-tension systems.

Tensioning Force

Cables tensioned to 75% of ultimate tensile strength with hydraulic jacks and load cells for precision.

Protection System

Multi-layer corrosion protection including sheathing, grouting, and corrosion inhibitors.

Quality Control

Comprehensive testing and documentation including elongation measurements and load verification.
