Why Pickleball is Booming

Pickleball has exploded in popularity across Utah and the entire country. This paddle sport combines elements of tennis, badminton, and ping-pong, making it accessible to players of all ages and skill levels. The smaller court size, slower ball speed, and emphasis on strategy over power has made pickleball the sport of choice for active communities.

With over 4.8 million players nationwide and growing, pickleball courts are in high demand. Whether you're a recreational player looking to practice at home or a community developer planning a commercial complex, investing in a pickleball court is a smart choice that will provide years of enjoyment and value.

Regulation Court Specifications

Official Court Dimensions

Every pickleball court we build adheres to USA Pickleball Association standards with precise 20' × 44' playing area dimensions. The court includes a 7-foot non-volley zone (the "kitchen") on each side of the net, creating the strategic elements that make pickleball unique.

  • Playing surface: 20 feet × 44 feet
  • Total recommended space: 30 feet × 60 feet
  • Net height: 36 inches at ends, 34 inches at center
  • Non-volley zone: 7 feet from net on each side
  • Service areas: Two 15' × 10' rectangles per side
  • Centerline divides court into left and right service areas

Frequently Asked Questions

How much space do I need for a pickleball court?

While the playing area is 20' × 44', we recommend a minimum total area of 30' × 60' to allow adequate out-of-bounds space. For optimal play, 34' × 64' provides comfortable clearance areas around the court.

Can I convert my tennis court to pickleball?

Absolutely! Tennis courts can accommodate multiple pickleball courts. One tennis court can fit four pickleball courts. We can add pickleball lines while maintaining the tennis lines, or completely resurface for dedicated pickleball use.

What's the typical construction timeline?

Most residential pickleball courts are completed in 5-7 business days after base preparation. This includes excavation, base installation, surface application, line marking, and net installation. Weather and site conditions may affect timing.

Do you handle permits and HOA approvals?

Yes, we assist with the permit process and provide professional drawings and specifications needed for HOA or city approval. Our experience with local regulations helps ensure smooth approval processes.

What maintenance is required for pickleball courts?

Pickleball courts require minimal maintenance. Regular cleaning, annual inspection for cracks, and periodic line refreshing (every 3-5 years) will keep your court in excellent condition. We offer maintenance programs for hassle-free upkeep.
