Design Considerations

Indoor Court Design Factors

Indoor courts require special attention to environmental factors that don't affect outdoor courts. Our design process addresses these considerations from the start.

Critical Factors:

  • Ventilation: Proper air circulation prevents moisture buildup and ensures player comfort
  • Lighting: Uniform, glare-free lighting designed for sports visibility
  • Acoustics: Sound control to minimize noise transmission to other areas
  • Flooring: Appropriate surface systems for indoor conditions
  • Ceiling Height: Adequate clearance for ball trajectory and safety
  • Wall Protection: Impact-resistant wall systems and safety padding

Pro Tip

Consider installing retractable nets for multi-use spaces, allowing the area to serve multiple purposes when not used for sports.
