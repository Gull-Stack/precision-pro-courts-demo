Professional Court Repair & Maintenance

Problem Identification

Common Court Issues We Fix

Recognizing problems early helps prevent costly repairs and maintains optimal playing conditions.

Surface Cracks

Thermal expansion, settling, or substrate movement causing linear cracks in the playing surface.

Solution: Professional crack repair with flexible sealants

Faded or Worn Lines

UV exposure, traffic, and weather causing line paint to fade or wear away over time.

Solution: Precision line repainting with UV-resistant paint

Surface Deterioration

General wear from use, weather, and age causing rough texture, color fading, or performance loss.

Solution: Surface restoration or complete resurfacing

Drainage Issues

Standing water, poor slope, or clogged drainage affecting court usability and surface integrity.

Solution: Drainage system repair and slope correction

Net System Problems

Worn hardware, damaged nets, or adjustment issues affecting game quality and safety.

Solution: Hardware replacement and system optimization
