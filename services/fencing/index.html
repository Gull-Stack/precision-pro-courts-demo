Why Court Fencing Matters

Court fencing is more than a boundary — it's an essential component that enhances safety, contains play, and provides the professional appearance that makes any court feel complete. Whether you're building a backyard court or a commercial facility, proper fencing protects your investment and improves the playing experience.

Utah's diverse landscapes and weather conditions require fencing solutions that withstand wind, temperature variations, and UV exposure while maintaining their appearance and functionality year after year.

Fencing Types & Materials

Galvanized Chain-Link

The most cost-effective and durable option, galvanized chain-link provides excellent ball containment and security while allowing visibility and airflow.

  • Heavy-duty galvanized steel construction
  • Rust and corrosion resistance
  • Multiple gauge options available
  • Cost-effective installation
  • Long-term durability

Vinyl-Coated Chain-Link

Enhanced chain-link with vinyl coating provides superior weather resistance and aesthetic appeal while maintaining the durability of steel construction.

  • Color options including green, black, and brown
  • Superior weather and UV protection
  • Enhanced aesthetic appearance
  • Reduced maintenance requirements
  • Professional facility appearance

Privacy & Security Options

Solid panel fencing and enhanced security features for courts requiring complete privacy or additional security measures.

  • Solid vinyl or composite panels
  • Privacy slat insertions
  • Security gate systems
  • Access control integration
  • Windscreen compatibility

Height Standards & Specifications

Proper fence height is crucial for effective ball containment and player safety. We install fencing that meets sport-specific requirements and local building codes.

Standard Height Options

  • 10 feet: Standard for tennis and recreational courts
  • 12 feet: Enhanced ball containment for active play
  • 8 feet: Minimum height for most residential applications
  • 6 feet: Basic containment for lower activity areas

Sport-Specific Requirements

  • Tennis: 10-12 feet recommended
  • Pickleball: 8-10 feet typically sufficient
  • Basketball: 8-12 feet depending on usage
  • Multi-sport: 10-12 feet for versatility

Frequently Asked Questions

What height fence do I need for my court?

Most residential courts use 10-foot fencing for effective ball containment. Tennis courts may benefit from 12-foot heights, while pickleball courts can often use 8-10 feet. We assess your specific needs based on court size, usage, and local requirements.

Can I add fencing to an existing court?

Absolutely! We frequently add fencing to existing courts that were built without enclosures. We carefully assess the site conditions and design fencing that complements your existing court while providing the containment and security you need.

Do I need permits for court fencing?

Most communities require permits for fencing over 6 feet in height. We help navigate the permit process and provide professional drawings and specifications needed for approval. HOA approval may also be required in some areas.

How long does fence installation take?

Most residential court fencing is completed in 3-5 business days, depending on fence height, gates, and accessories. Weather conditions and site access can affect timing, but we work efficiently to minimize disruption.

What's the difference between galvanized and vinyl-coated fencing?

Galvanized fencing offers excellent durability at lower cost with a traditional silver appearance. Vinyl-coated fencing provides superior weather resistance, color options, and a more professional appearance at slightly higher cost.
