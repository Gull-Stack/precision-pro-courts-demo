The Multi-Sport Advantage

Multi-sport courts represent the ultimate in recreational versatility. Instead of dedicating space to a single sport, these innovative designs allow families and communities to enjoy multiple activities on one professionally constructed surface.

In Utah, where outdoor recreation is a way of life, multi-sport courts have become increasingly popular for their ability to accommodate different interests, age groups, and skill levels. From backyard family recreation to community center facilities, these courts provide maximum value for your investment.

Popular Sport Combinations

The most requested combination, perfect for families with varied interests. Basketball hoops at regulation height with pickleball courts marked in contrasting colors.

  • Year-round activity options

Tennis + Pickleball

Convert existing tennis courts or design new facilities that serve both racquet sports with clearly defined playing areas and proper net systems.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many sports can you fit on one court?

This depends on court size and sport compatibility. A full basketball court can accommodate four pickleball courts, tennis and pickleball can share space efficiently, and most courts can handle 2-4 sports with proper line configuration and equipment planning.

Do different sports interfere with each other?

With proper design, sports can coexist beautifully. We use color-coded line systems, strategic equipment placement, and clear visual separation to ensure each sport maintains its integrity and playability within the shared space.

Are multi-sport courts more expensive than single-sport courts?

Multi-sport courts typically cost slightly more due to additional line marking and equipment, but they provide significantly more value per square foot. The cost per sport decreases dramatically compared to building separate courts for each activity.

How do you handle different net heights for different sports?

We install versatile net systems that can be quickly adjusted for different sports, or provide separate net posts for sports requiring different heights. Portable and semi-permanent solutions are both available depending on your usage patterns.

Can I add sports to an existing court later?

In most cases, yes! Existing courts can often accommodate additional sports with line marking, equipment installation, and minor modifications. We evaluate existing surfaces and provide recommendations for expansion possibilities.
