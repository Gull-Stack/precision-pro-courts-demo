Why Quality Coatings Matter

Court coatings are far more than cosmetic enhancements — they're protective systems that shield your court from Utah's harsh climate while providing the playing characteristics that make sports enjoyable and safe.

From the intense UV radiation in southern Utah to the freeze-thaw cycles of northern regions, quality coatings protect your investment while ensuring consistent ball bounce, proper traction, and professional appearance that lasts for years.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I recoat my court?

Most courts need recoating every 4-6 years depending on usage, maintenance, and weather exposure. High-traffic commercial courts may need more frequent attention, while well-maintained residential courts can last longer between coatings.

Can you change the color of my existing court?

Yes! Court recoating is the perfect time to update colors, add multi-sport lines, or create custom designs. We can completely transform your court's appearance while improving its performance characteristics.

What's the difference between regular paint and court coating systems?

Court coating systems are specially formulated for sports surfaces with specific texture, durability, and performance requirements. Regular paint cannot provide the ball bounce, traction, and weather resistance needed for quality courts.

How long does the coating process take?

Most coating projects take 3-5 days including preparation, application, and curing time. Weather conditions affect the timeline, and we schedule work during optimal conditions for best results.

Do you warranty your coating work?

Yes, we provide warranties on both materials and workmanship. Warranty terms depend on the coating system selected and usage conditions. We stand behind our work and use only proven, high-quality products.
